Meet your chiropractor

Stuart got into chiropractic the same way most patients do: an injury nothing else fixed.

Raised on the Isle of Wight, Stuart picked up a football injury at sixteen that physio and rest couldn't shift. A short course of chiropractic had him back playing within weeks. He trained at the McTimoney College of Chiropractic and has since treated patients across three countries.

Every plan is tailored to you, and he explains what he's doing and why, in plain English.
